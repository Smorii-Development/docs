Smorii is a Discord staff management bot built for roleplay and community teams.

It combines staff tools, support systems, moderation, and website features in one place.

## What Smorii focuses on

Smorii is mainly built for servers that need structure.

That includes:

- Staff teams
- Support teams
- Roleplay communities
- Roblox-linked communities
- Management-heavy servers

## What makes it different

Smorii is not just one feature.

It combines:

- Discord commands
- Config panels
- Staff tracking systems
- Website dashboards
- Roblox account linking
- Support workflows like tickets

## Main feature groups

- Staff systems: infractions, LOAs, shifts, sessions, feedback
- Support systems: tickets, suggestions
- Utility systems: custom commands, auto responders, welcome flows
- External systems: website login, Roblox verification, ERLC

## How to think about setup

You do not need to use everything.

Most servers start by enabling only the modules they actually need, then expand later.
